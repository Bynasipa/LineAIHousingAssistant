# Privacy Policy

This LINE bot does not collect or store personal data permanently.

## What we collect

* User ID from LINE platform
* Messages sent to the bot

## How we use data

* To respond to user messages
* To provide chatbot functionality and apartment recommendations

## Data storage

We do not store personal data long-term. Data is processed only during interaction.

## Third parties

We do not sell or share user data with third parties.
